The Roots

Where I Come From

January 29, 2009

Birai Khurd, Gorakhpur

I was born in a village so small, it barely appears on maps. Where the roads turn to mud when it rains. Where electricity is a fickle friend.

Statistics said I would become another forgotten face.

But statistics don't account for the fire that burns in certain souls.

The Foundation

My Parents

Ramsahai Vishwakarma — my father. His hands were rough from labor, his back bent from years of carrying burdens. He never told me he loved me in words. But he showed it in every sacrifice.

Lalita Devi — my mother. Her faith in me was so absolute that it became my foundation.

October 6, 2025

The Day the World Went Dark

The day my father died.

I cannot write these words without my hands trembling.

"Time is the only non-renewable resource. Legacy is what outlasts you."

I made a promise: I would not simply survive. I would build.

2022 — Age 13

The First Line of Code

An old salvaged computer. Its screen flickered with uncertainty. But to me, it was a portal to infinite possibility.

printf("Hello, World!");

In that moment, I understood: technology is not something we use—it is something we shape.
